Welcome to Villa Victorina El Roque

Villa Victorina El Roque is an elegant private vacation villa located in the charming village of El Roque, Fuerteventura. This beautiful property offers three spacious bedrooms and three bathrooms, perfect for families or groups seeking comfort and privacy.

The villa features a heated outdoor pool, a terrace with outdoor seating area, and private parking. With WiFi, air conditioning throughout, and a fully equipped modern kitchen, you have everything needed for an unforgettable vacation in the Canary Islands.

Located just 2.2 km from Playa del Castillo beach and 42 km from Fuerteventura Airport, Villa Victorina El Roque combines the tranquility of a private home with easy access to the island's most beautiful locations.

Location

El Roque, Fuerteventura

Villa Victorina is located in El Roque, next to the charming village of El Cotillo on the beautiful island of Fuerteventura. The island is renowned for its endless beaches, crystal-clear waters, and year-round pleasant weather - perfect for sun lovers and water sports enthusiasts.

The villa is situated in a peaceful area with easy access to the island's most beautiful beaches. Perfect for those seeking tranquility and relaxation, with all amenities within reach.

Places to Visit

Beaches of El Cotillo

El Cotillo offers two worlds in one village. On the east side lie calm turquoise lagoons — perfect for families with children. Shallow water, white sand, barely any current. On the west coast, Atlantic waves roll in with some of the best surf spots in Fuerteventura. Whether you're a beginner or advanced, conditions are ideal nearly year-round.

Photo Luca Iaconelli / Unsplash

El Cotillo Village

A fishing village that has kept its character. Fresh fish straight from the harbour, small tapas bars, sunset on a terrace with sea views. No flashy boulevards, but whitewashed streets and a relaxed Canarian atmosphere. In the evening, stroll from restaurant to wine bar — no crowds, no stress.

Photo Rudi Bloemmen / Unsplash

Corralejo & Isla de Lobos

A 20-minute drive takes you to Corralejo: livelier, with shops, a marina, and a launchpad for adventure. From the harbour, take the boat to Isla de Lobos — a protected nature reserve with crystal-clear water and hiking trails — or Lanzarote for a day trip full of lava landscapes and César Manrique architecture.

Photo Marcreation / Unsplash

Dunes of Corralejo

The dunes of the Parque Natural de Corralejo are unique in Europe. Kilometres of white sand, azure ocean, an almost desert-like landscape. Perfect for a morning walk, photo shoot, or simply to pause and be still. It feels like you're on another planet.

Photo Sam Williams / Unsplash

Playa de Cofete

Pure, rugged, and breathtaking. One of the most spectacular beaches of the Canary Islands. No hotels. No crowds. Just mountains, ocean, and endless space. The drive there over unpaved roads makes the adventure complete.

Photo Michael Baccin / Unsplash
