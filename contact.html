Location

El Roque, Fuerteventura

Villa Victorina is located in the quiet village of El Roque, next to El Cotillo on the northwest coast of Fuerteventura, Canary Islands, Spain.

Getting Here

  • Fuerteventura Airport (FUE) — approx. 42 km / 35 min drive
  • Playa del Castillo beach — 2.2 km
  • El Cotillo village — 3 km
  • Corralejo — 20 km

Private parking is available at the villa.
